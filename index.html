Basic Rules & Information

  • Begin playing: Open the deck tab and draw up to 3 challenges to your hand.
  • Claiming: Enable the Claim toggle, then tap a neighborhood. Tap again to remove if you made a mistake.
  • Battle Challenge: Battle an opposing neighborhood if you own 1/3 of surrounding neighborhoods. Use the Lock button to lock ownership for the winner.
  • Powerups: Complete a ⚡ challenge to earn powerups. Use them from the Powerups button.
    • Discard hand & draw new cards
    • Swap a challenge with an opponent
    • Start a battle without 1/3 adjacency
    • Freeze opposing teams for 5 minutes [ONE USE PER TEAM]
  • Dual-threshold challenges: Some challenges show a ⚡? tag — completing at the higher threshold earns a powerup.
  • Remember: Draw 1 new challenge after each completion. Max hand size is 3.
  • Full Rules: rules document
  • Note: You must sign in and join a team to edit the map.
