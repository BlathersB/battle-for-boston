Basic Rules & Information

  • Begin playing: Open the deck tab and draw 3 challenges to your hand.
  • Claiming: Tap a neighborhood with your color upon completing a challenge, tap again to remove claim if you made a mistake.
  • Battle Challenge: Battle an opposing neighborhood if you own 1/3 of the surrounding neighborhoods, draw battle challenge card if you are initiating. Use the lock button to lock ownership for winner of challenge
  • Powerups: If you complete a challenge with a powerup, open the Shop window to redeem it.
  • Remember: Draw 1 new challenge after each one you complete. You cannot discard challenges aside from earning a powerup or pulling a duplicate
  • Full Rules: Consult rules document for full detailed explanations
  • Note: You must be signed into a Google account in order to play and save your progress.
