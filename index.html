Rules Info

  • Begin playing: Open the deck tab and draw 7 challenges
  • Claiming: Tap a neighborhood with your color upon completing a challenge, tap again to remove claim if you made a mistake.
  • Battle Challenge: Battle an opposing neighborhood if you own 1/3 of the surrounding neighborhoods, draw battle challenge card if you are initiating. Use the lock button to lock ownership for winner of challenge
  • Powerups: If you complete a challenge with a powerup, choose one of the following:
    • Discard your entire hard, draw new cards
    • Swap one challenge with an opponent (their deck will be revealed, you choose the card)
    • Start a battle without controlling 1/3 of adjacent neighborhoods
    • Freeze opposing teams for 5 minutes outside of battle [ONLY ONE USE PER TEAM PER GAME]
  • Battles: Use the Battle Challenge slot for high-stakes neighborhood disputes
  • Full Rules: Consult rules document for the full detailed rules
  • Note: You must be signed into an account with a whitelisted email in order to play
